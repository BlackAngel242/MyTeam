## 2026-10-29 - Tooltips for icon-only buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons often lack visual tooltips, causing poor discoverability for sighted users who don't know what the icon represents, even if ARIA attributes exist for screen readers.
**Action:** Always add localized `title` attributes to icon-only buttons in addition to ARIA labels or visually hidden spans, providing a hover tooltip for better UX.
