# Code of Conduct

[Français](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | **English**

## Our Pledge

As members, contributors, and leaders of MyTeam (The Agent Office), we pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free, welcoming, and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We expect everyone participating in the project to maintain a calm, courteous, and respectful demeanor toward the work of others.

## Expected Behavior

- Demonstrating kindness, empathy, and active listening during discussions.
- Accepting constructive feedback with humility.
- Prioritizing what is best for the overall community and for project users.
- Respecting observed facts and data truth, which form the bedrock of the project's philosophy.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Demeaning, insulting, derogatory, or discriminatory language and comments.
- Harassment in any form, whether public or private.
- Publishing private information of others without explicit consent.
- Any conduct that disrupts collaboration and constructive community work.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported confidentially through GitHub private reporting on the repository (under "Security" or "Report a vulnerability / Private vulnerability reporting").

All reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and discreetly by the project maintainers.
